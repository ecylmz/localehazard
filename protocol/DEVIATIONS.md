# Decision log of the PostgreSQL experiments

This file is the part of the study's append-only decision log that concerns the PostgreSQL experiments. Each row records a change to the design or the implementation, the date, and the results that were known at that time. Rows marked POST_HOC describe experiments that were added after earlier results were known.

The component names refer to the experiments of this package. The drift rows of October 2026 belong to the emulated drift experiment (`dbms/run_dbms_experiment.py`). The design of that experiment is in `AMENDMENT_A2.md`. Older script comments call this experiment "RQ2" or "RQ3", after the numbering of the larger study. In the paper, it is part of RQ1.
